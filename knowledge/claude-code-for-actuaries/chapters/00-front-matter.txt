 Executive Summary
 Actuaries spend a considerable share of their time on repetitive technical tasks or mundane work with little
 value added: writing Python, R or VBA code, cleaning portfolio data, producing regulatory reports,
 documenting models, testing developments. These are low-leverage tasks that consume scarce,
 expensive expertise.

 Claude Code changes that equation. It is an AI agent that works directly in your environment: it writes
 code, runs it, reads its own errors and corrects itself until the result is right. A VBA valuation function that
 takes a day to develop and debug is generated in minutes. Year-end technical documentation that takes
 days is produced in one session. An ALM dashboard or an RBC calculator is built in a day, without waiting
 on IT.

 This guide covers the full range of actuarial practice in North America: life and annuities (ALM, assets,
 modeling, risk measurement and capital, model validation, statutory valuation, product development,
 investments, health and group benefits), property & casualty (pricing, reserving, reporting) and
 reinsurance. Each use case is presented with its business context, a description of what Claude Code will
 design, an exhaustive ready-to-paste prompt, and the expected inputs and outputs.

 Ten of these use cases ship with a downloadable companion project folder: the dataset, the CLAUDE.md,
 the ready-to-paste prompts, and reference results to check your run against. The full list, with download
 links, is in chapter 1.

 Beyond business use cases, the guide shows how to build internal tools in a day, how to create AI agents
 able to answer your teams' questions about your models, and how to frame all of it with security,
 confidentiality and governance rules suited to insurance data and to the expectations of US and Canadian
 regulators.


   Note: The goal of this guide is not to do AI for AI's sake. It is to make you faster and more rigorous on the
   tasks that matter, while keeping the actuary at the center of every decision.
